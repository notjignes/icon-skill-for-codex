# UC Icons Contract

## Output layers

- Request a high-quality, opaque `2048x1536` PNG for every new generation or base edit.
- Preserve a valid native 4:3 master without upscaling.
- Derive a `1024x768` PNG delivery on exact `#f5f5f5`.
- Keep one high-resolution physical master per approved bank asset; do not store a bank delivery duplicate.
- Preserve exact legacy masters for exact export and base editing.

## Subject and composition

- Render one centered tactile 3D micro-object or character bust.
- Prefer a familiar real-world silhouette over decorative detail.
- Keep one dominant mass and no more than one subordinate service cue unless the approved composition family is explicitly `grouped` or `carrier-contents`.
- Show the complete silhouette with comfortable 4:3 margins.
- Avoid rooms, lifestyle scenes, tabletops, hands, extra people, packaging, unrelated props, readable text, logos, branding, barcodes, and watermarks.

## View taxonomy

Keep camera view separate from canvas layout:

- `view`: `front`, `side`, `front-3q`, `slight-top`
- `layout_axis`: `compact`, `vertical`, `horizontal`
- `projection`: `near-orthographic`, `mild-perspective`
- `composition_family`: `single`, `object-cue`, `carrier-contents`, `grouped`

Use a true side view for vehicles and side-profile tools. Use slight-top only when an open top or upper surface is necessary for recognition. Do not force all wide furniture into a vehicle-style side camera.

## Material and lighting grammar

- Use believable but edited material realism.
- Keep matte plastic molded and clean; fabric tactile but simplified; wood lightly finished; brushed metal directional but restrained; chrome reflective without mirror clutter; glass and liquid readable against `#f5f5f5`.
- Use soft front-biased studio light, broad fill, gentle contrast, and controlled highlights.
- Keep bevels soft and approachable without toy-like exaggeration.
- Use palette roles: dominant material, secondary material, functional neutral, and an optional recognition accent.

## Shadow authority

For a new-from-scratch master:

> No external cast, contact, drop, or floor shadow, glow, or ambient grounding pool. Internal self-shadow, occlusion, and material shading remain allowed.

For exact legacy exports and ordinary legacy base edits, preserve the selected source treatment unless the user explicitly asks to remove it. A legacy edit does not become a reusable generation reference without separate restaging and approval under the new-reference contract.

## Reference behavior

- Routine calls automatically use approved internal references without asking for approval.
- Attach at most three references and at most one per needed role: `form`, `material`, `composition`, `style`.
- Every attachment must have positive semantic and structural value; abstain when none qualifies.
- A user-provided reference is request-scoped and has precedence for that run only.
- Never add, learn from, or promote a supplied reference or successful output automatically.

## Promotion authority

Only an explicit skill-improvement request activates candidate handling. Promotion requires:

1. Valid high-resolution 4:3 opaque PNG and portable provenance.
2. Exact exposed `#f5f5f5` background and valid path/hash checks.
3. Correct subject, view, layout, composition, materials, and small-size recognition.
4. Review against at least six nearby approved peers at full size and 96/64/48/32 px.
5. Two independent approved visual reviews.
6. A passed held-out transfer test whose arm means are derived from complete per-run
   blind ratings, with no form leakage or critical regression.
7. Explicit owner approval and note.

Each logical reference is approved for exactly one tested role. A master may back
multiple role-specific logical records without duplicating its physical PNG. Runtime
cross-family permissions are derived only from held-out subjects that clear the blind
transfer score margin.

Candidates, rejected assets, and quarantined assets remain outside the installable skill and are never attached at runtime.
